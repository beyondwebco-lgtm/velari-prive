THE DAILY CEREMONY

The ANAGÈNESE Ritual

A moment of deliberate sensory calm. Designed to transform hair and scalp application into an intentional daily discipline.

01
PURIFICATION & PARTING

The Scalp Reset

The ritual is optimal on clean, dry or lightly towel-damp hair. Part the hair in four parallel lines from the frontal hairline back toward the crown using your fingertips or a wide-toothed horn comb.

Sensory note: Ensure the scalp is free of heavy silicone styling films, enabling unrestricted follicular contact.

02
CALIBRATED MEASURE

The 1.0ml Micro-Dose

Depress the silver rubber bulb once to draw exactly 1.0ml of the crystal-clear elixir into the borosilicate pipette. ANAGÈNESE is formulated at therapeutic concentration: a single calibrated pipette is sufficient for the complete scalp perimeter.

Tactile note: The elixir is silky, non-sticky, and instantly breaks into aqueous hydration upon contact with skin temperature.

03
FOCUSED DISTRIBUTION

Targeted Sectioning

Incline the head slightly backward. Glide the pipette tip directly above the scalp without dragging, releasing micro-droplets along each parted furrow:

  • Three droplets across the frontal hairline
  • Four droplets along the central crown
  • Two droplets across each temporal zone and nape
04
SOMATIC STIMULATION

Acupressure Activation

Using the pads of both thumbs and four fingers, apply firm, circular pressure beginning at the base of the skull and travelling upward to the vertex. Maintain this gentle mechanical massage for 90 to 120 seconds.

Finish: The elixir absorbs entirely within 3 minutes. Style as desired. Zero residue, zero greasiness, zero flattening of hair root volume.

RITUAL CADENCE

Harmonious Daily Integration

ANAGÈNESE is formulated for daily continuity. Consistency of ritual cultivates optimal scalp micro-climate health.

MATINAL RITUAL (MORNING)

Protection & Volume

Apply 10 minutes prior to blow-drying or styling. The weightless formulation acts as an invisible shield against urban environmental aggressors and oxidative pollutants without inhibiting your styling products.

NOCTURNAL RITUAL (EVENING)

Deep Night Recovery

Apply immediately after evening shower or prior to sleep. During the nocturnal regenerative cycle, cellular permeability peaks. Leaves no oil mark or scent transfer on luxury silk or linen pillowcases.
