THE DAILY CEREMONY

The Ritual Preview.

A quiet moment of tactile renewal designed for morning clarity or nocturnal calm.

01

The Parting

Part dry or lightly towel-damp hair into clean sections, preparing the scalp surface for intimate contact.

02

The Calibrated Micro-Dose

Depress the silver bulb once to draw 1.0ml of crystal-clear elixir into the borosilicate pipette. Disperse drop by drop along parted lines.

03

The Aqueous Absorption

Glide fingertips gently over the scalp with circular micro-pressures. The elixir absorbs weightlessly within seconds without coating or oiliness.
