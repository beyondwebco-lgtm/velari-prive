DAILY APPLICATION PROTOCOL

A Considered Personal Ritual

ANAGÈNESE is formulated to integrate seamlessly into morning or evening hair care rituals without residue, greasiness, or weight.

01 PREPARATION

Cleanse & Part

Section dry or towel-damp hair along the natural crown line. Ensure scalp surface is receptive and unburdened by heavy styling polymers.

02 PRECISION DOSAGE

Pipette Draw

Draw exactly 1.0ml of the crystal-clear elixir using the graduated silver pipette. The formula delivers concentrated micronutrients in measured drops.

03 DISTRIBUTION

Direct Micro-Droplets

Release targeted micro-droplets along parted sections directly onto the scalp surface, concentrating on the hairline, crown, and areas of focus.

04 ACTIVATION

Acupressure Motion

Gently press fingertips in circular motions for 90 seconds. The elixir absorbs immediately without altering hair volume or texture.
