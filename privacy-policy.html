Privacy Notice

VELARI PRIVÉ operates upon principles of uncompromising discretion, confidentiality, and data integrity.

1. Principles of Discretion

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize patron or applicant data. Enquiries submitted through the Professional Expression of Interest or Private Discovery channels are received solely by authorized representatives of the Velari Privé Directorship.

2. Analytics & Non-Personally Identifiable Measurement

Our website utilizes aggregate web analytics to optimize performance, layout stability, and user experience. We strictly prohibit the transmission of personal identifiers (such as names, telephone numbers, and email addresses) to analytics platforms.

3. Data Security & Storage

All transmitted information is encrypted using transport layer security (HTTPS). Records are stored within secure, access-controlled environments in compliance with Australian and international data protection standards.

4. Direct Contact

For inquiries regarding privacy, data verification, or record deletion, contact the House at: concierge@velariprive.au.
