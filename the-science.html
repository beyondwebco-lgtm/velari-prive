FORMULATION & PHYSIOLOGY

The Science Behind ANAGÈNESE.

An exploration of formulation, intention, and scalp care.

Cosmetic scalp care is often clouded by aggressive clinical promises and confusing technical terms. At VELARI PRIVÉ, we believe in clarity: formulating with verified cosmetic actives, respecting natural physiology, and creating an intimate daily discipline.

PHYSIOLOGICAL PERSPECTIVE

Understanding the Scalp Environment

The scalp is skin — yet it carries a uniquely dense follicular network, active sebaceous glands, and its own delicate microbiome.

MOISTURE & COMFORT

Hydration Equilibrium

Environmental fluctuations, hard tap water, and thermal styling deplete surface hydration. Maintaining moisture comfort prevents tightness and flaking without triggering reactive oil production.

SURFACE HARMONY

The Protective Barrier

The stratum corneum and delicate acid mantle protect deeper layers against daily pollution and oxidation. Formulations must respect physiological pH (~5.5) rather than stripping surface lipids.

DAILY DISCIPLINE

Considered Routines

Effective scalp care is not an emergency intervention, but a consistent ritual. Gentle daily application allows active cosmetic compounds to nurture hair strands from their foundation.

Cosmetic Transparency: Scalp care products support cosmetic comfort, moisture balance, and surface appearance. ANAGÈNESE does not alter genetic follicular architecture or diagnose, cure, or treat pathological scalp disorders.

FORMULATION RIGOUR

The Australian Formulation Approach.

Biomimetic lightweight delivery meets botanical curiosity.

Developed under Australian formulation guidance, ANAGÈNESE rejects traditional heavy hair oils that clog follicular pores. Instead, our chemists engineered an ultra-refined, micro-aqueous delivery system that releases active hydration upon skin contact.

Every ingredient is selected for biological tolerance and sensory delight: no heavy silicones, zero parabens, no synthetic dyes, and no drying alcohols.
